## Analogues

1. **US 2000 to 2002. Closest, but only fragments.** Dalio scores "USA 2000" yes on all seven bubble tests (dalio ch02). IG widened 117bp and HY 431bp, with the peak about 558 days into the recession (willer ch03, Table 3.5). Credit beat equities in that recession. In 2002 two large failures pulled credit lower while lending conditions improved (willer ch11). No book describes the telecom issuers or vendor financing.
2. **US 1927 to 1929.** A technology boom, Fed tightening, and leverage built outside banks (dalio ch08). The leverage funded stock purchases, not company investment.
3. **Subprime 2007 to 2008.** The weakest borrowers lost market access first, and risk came back to banks through credit lines (cassidy ch19, ch22; claessens ch03). The debt was household debt with short-term funding.
4. **1998.** A spread shock with no recession (axilrod ch06; gurevich ch05). The Fed was on hold, not hiking.
5. **Baring 1890.** A railway boom, a higher centre rate and a failed bond issue (eichengreen ch07). The borrower was a foreign government.

## Move sizes the books state

- IG widening in recessions: 67 to 486bp, median 124bp. HY: 431, 785 and 1,602bp. The IG peak came a median 352 days after the recession began (willer ch03, Table 3.5).
- HY defaults peak at 10 to 15%, after the recession ends (willer ch11).
- In acute stress, single-A and Baa bonds "can do as badly as Ba and B" (willer ch11).
- Fallen angels enter HY about 150bp cheap to peers, then beat BB bonds over the next 12 months (willer ch11).

## Fed reaction the books state

- The gap from the last hike to the first cut was 1 to 18 months across eight cycles (gurevich ch02).
- No emergency cut in the books came while the Fed was still hiking (case agent's reading of gurevich ch04).
- A bear market without a recession did not need a cut (willer ch03).

## Base rates

- "Only a minority of credit booms end in a financial crisis". Longer booms end in crisis more often (claessens ch01).
- The first debt problems appear about six months before the economy peaks (dalio ch02).

## Adjustments (additive)

1. **Confidence in "repricing, not a break": slightly higher for now.** The breaks in the books needed leverage inside finance with short-term funding. The evidence packs do not show that yet.
2. **The key unknown moves to the lenders.** How do the banks, insurers and private funds that hold AI loans fund themselves? No pack answers this.
3. **Proposal to the PM: the grid's hard-landing moves may be too small.** The grid has IG +56bp and HY +248bp in six months. The books give a median IG move of 124bp and a smallest HY move of 431bp, but over about a year. I did not change the grid.
4. **Dalio's sequence fits the first steps.** Risky borrowers lose access and spreads rise while the Fed still hikes. That sequence ended in a downturn in his template. So "repricing" is not a safe end state.
