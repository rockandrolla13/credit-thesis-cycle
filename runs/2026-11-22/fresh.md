# Fresh causal chain (phase A)

Written 2026-11-23 from observations and method material only. No case chapter and no past-episode evidence was read before this file was frozen.

## Driver
Debt-financed AI capital spending by a few highly rated companies, during a Fed hiking cycle.

## Mechanism
1. A few AA and A rated companies issue a large share of new debt. Hyperscalers are about 14% of non-financial IG issuance in 2026, against about 2% three years ago (@junkbondinvest, 18 Nov 2026). Goldman counts $296bn of hyperscaler debt in 2026 (web search, 23 Nov 2026). The Fed counts more than $680bn of advanced-computing debt to October, a wider definition.
2. Supply in the top ratings lifts top-rated spreads against lower-rated spreads. AA spreads are 0.58 of BBB spreads, against a three-year mean of 0.476 (FRED BAMLC0A2CAA, BAMLC0A4CBBB, 19 Nov 2026).
3. Fed hikes raise the yield on safe assets. Buyers then need less weak credit to reach a yield target (POLITICO Morning Money, 6 Oct 2026; @lisaabramowicz1, 20 Nov 2026).
4. The market sorts AI borrowers by support. Debt with a big-tech contract weakened least (@nodiligence, 21 Nov 2026). CoreWeave CDS passed 1,200bp (Fixed + Floating, 17 Nov 2026). Hayford and Malliaris: shocks amplify when borrowers lose access to financing (ch02).
5. Borrowers without bond access use bank loans and private credit. That debt sits outside the bond indices. ETFs hold 3% of IG data-centre bonds (WSJ, 19 Nov 2026). Private AI loans paid 0.1 point more than other private loans (BIS study, via Social Capital, 15 Oct 2026). Schulte: count non-deposit lenders or you miss a stealth rise (ch02).
6. Wider spreads slow projects, and slower projects need less debt (Slok via @lisaabramowicz1, 24 Oct 2026). This is a balancing loop. Willer: supply is endogenous, and issuance follows returns (ch11).
7. Two delayed risks remain. Bank loans must become bonds later (@nodiligence, 10 Nov 2026, one source). A large issuer one notch above junk could be downgraded (Finimize, 16 Oct 2026).

## Operational axis
AA to BBB option-adjusted spread ratio: FRED BAMLC0A2CAA divided by BAMLC0A4CBBB. Daily history from November 2023.
Level 0.581 on 19 Nov 2026. Three-year range 0.388 to 0.616. Mean 0.476. The level is 2.1 standard deviations above the mean and at the 92nd percentile. The peak was on 2 Sep 2026.

## What is priced, from the axis
- Supply premium in AA today: (0.581 - 0.476) x 117bp = about 12bp of the 68bp AA spread.
- The grid puts the ratio near 0.64 if 2027 supply arrives in full. The gap from the mean is then 0.164. Today's gap is 0.105. So the top ratings price roughly two thirds of that case. This is a judgement, not a measurement.
- Weak credit is repriced. CCC spreads are 1,361bp, the three-year high. HY is at the 92nd percentile.
- The IG index is at the 79th percentile at 94bp.
- Not visible in bond prices: debt held as bank loans and private credit.

## Dalio's productive-credit test and Cassidy's borrower mix
The method asks whether borrowed money earns enough to service the debt, and what share of borrowers must roll debt to pay interest. The packs give no measured answer. One post says three of the four largest spenders now spend more than they generate (The VC Corner, 21 Nov 2026, second-hand). This is an open question.
