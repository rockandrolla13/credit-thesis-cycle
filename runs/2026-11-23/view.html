How much AI debt supply do US credit spreads price?

Top-rated bond spreads price about two thirds of next year's AI debt supply, while bank loans, private credit and off-balance-sheet vehicles stay unpriced.

Every credit spread widened with the 20 Nov prints, and the axis rose to 0.590. No falsifier triggered and no probability moved. Two X accounts describe an equity rebound on 23 Nov, so these spreads may now be too wide.

As of 2026-11-23. Research only. Scenario probabilities are proposals.

Terms. Capex is capital spending. A spread is the extra yield that a bond pays over Treasuries. IG means investment grade, and HY means high yield. AA, A and BBB are rating tiers inside IG, and CCC is the weakest HY tier. One bp is one hundredth of a percentage point. The horizon is 2027-05-23.

The causal chain

Part of the supply effect lands in Treasury yields, in a size the memo cannot state. The IG index spread, at 98bp, shows little of it. A second bank met weak demand for a data-centre loan sale. Wider spreads also slow projects, and slower projects need less debt.

The operational axis

The axis is the AA spread divided by the BBB spread, from FRED. Today the AA spread is 72bp and the BBB spread is 122bp.

The green band is the three-year range. Today is 2.25 standard deviations above the mean. The grid puts the ratio near 0.65 if 2027 supply arrives in full.
