# Glossary: In Search of Distress Risk (Campbell, Hilscher, Szilagyi)

**Bankruptcy indicator** — equals one in the month a firm files under Chapter 7 or Chapter 11; from Chava and Jarrow (2004); January 1963 to December 1998 (Sec 2)
**Best model** — the authors' eight-variable logit: NIMTAAVG, TLMTA, EXRETAVG, SIGMA, RSIZE, CASHMTA, MB, PRICE (Sec 3, Table 3 columns 4-6)
**BD** — face value of debt in the distance-to-default calculation: short-term debt plus one half of long-term book debt (Appendix)
**CASHMTA** — cash and short-term investments over market value of total assets; the liquidity measure (Sec 2, Appendix)
**DD (distance to default)** — Merton-based gap between asset value and face value of debt, scaled by asset volatility; uses a 0.06 equity premium proxy and T = 1 (Sec 3.2, Appendix)
**Distress premium** — the conjectured extra return for holding stocks of firms likely to fail; the paper finds the opposite sign (Sec 1, Sec 4)
**Dynamic logit** — monthly panel logit for the probability of failure next period, after Shumway (2001) and Chava and Jarrow (2004) (Sec 1, Sec 3)
**EXRET** — monthly log excess return over the S&P 500: log(1+R_i) - log(1+R_S&P500) (Sec 2, Appendix)
**EXRETAVG** — 12-month weighted average of EXRET with weights that halve each quarter, φ = 2^(-1/3) (Sec 3, eq 3)
**Failure indicator** — equals one if a firm files for bankruptcy, delists, or receives a D rating; January 1963 to December 2003 (Sec 2)
**Frailty** — the effect of shocks correlated across firms that a firm-level model misses (Sec 3.3)
**KRIS** — Kamakura Risk Information Services, the source of the bankruptcy and failure indicators (Sec 1)
**LS1090 / LS2080** — long the safest 10% (20%) of stocks, short the most distressed 10% (20%) (Sec 4, Table 6)
**Market value of total assets** — market equity plus book value of total liabilities (Sec 2)
**MB** — the firm's market-to-book ratio; enters as a correction factor and a modest overvaluation signal (Sec 2, Sec 3)
**McFadden's pseudo R2** — 1 - L1/L0, with L0 the log likelihood of a constant-only model (Sec 3)
**NIMTA** — net income over market value of total assets (Sec 2, Appendix)
**NIMTAAVG** — weighted average of the last four quarterly NIMTA values, weights halving each quarter (Sec 3, eq 2)
**NITA** — net income over adjusted book total assets; the traditional ratio (Sec 2, Appendix)
**Phat** — fitted probability of failure from the 12-month-ahead model; the portfolio sorting variable (Sec 4, Table 6)
**PRICE** — log price per share, truncated above at $15 (Sec 2, Sec 3)
**RSIZE** — log of firm market equity over total S&P 500 market value (Sec 2, Appendix)
**Shumway model** — the five-variable benchmark: NITA, TLTA, EXRET, SIGMA, RSIZE (Sec 3, Table 3 columns 1-3)
**SIGMA** — annualized standard deviation of daily stock returns over the past three months, centred on zero (Sec 2, Appendix)
**TLMTA** — total liabilities over market value of total assets (Sec 2, Appendix)
**TLTA** — total liabilities over adjusted book total assets (Sec 2, Appendix)
**Total Assets (adjusted)** — book total assets adjusted by 10% of the gap between market and book equity, after Cohen, Polk and Vuolteenaho (2003); the sign is printed inconsistently (Sec 2, Appendix)
**Winsorising rule** — replace values below the 5th percentile or above the 95th percentile of the cross-section with those percentiles (Sec 2)
