# Glossary: Trading Global Macro Markets

**55dma** — 55-day moving average, the authors' default signal parameter (Ch 2)
**200dma** — 200-day moving average, their main risk-management line for equities (Ch 3, Ch 8)
**AAII bull-bear** — individual investor survey. Below -20 is a contrarian buy (Ch 9)
**Active information ratio** — IR measured only while a rule holds a position (Ch 2)
**Backwardation / contango** — downward or upward sloping futures curve. Contango means negative roll yield (Ch 14)
**Bear flattening / bull steepening** — the normal curve moves in hiking and easing cycles (Ch 5)
**Bear steepening** — back-end yields rising most. Linked to fiscal fear or a Fed behind the curve (Ch 5)
**Blackout period** — two weeks before earnings when buybacks stop (Ch 9)
**Breakeven** — nominal minus real yield. The instrument for inflation views (Ch 5)
**Bubble (GMO definition)** — price more than two standard deviations above long-term real trend (Ch 8)
**CAPE** — cyclically adjusted P/E. Predicts 10-year returns, fails as a timing tool (Ch 2, Ch 8)
**Carry to vol** — implied yield over implied volatility, the FX carry ranking (Ch 12)
**CECID** — Citi data change index: releases vs their own prior 12 months (Ch 12)
**CESI** — Citi economic surprise index: releases vs consensus (Ch 6, Ch 9)
**Credit canary** — credit spreads warning ahead of equities (Ch 11)
**CTA** — commodity trading advisor, mostly trend following. The stable beta of macro funds (Ch 2, Ch 15)
**Curve carry** — short front, long back contracts of one commodity (Ch 14)
**Cyclicals vs defensives** — equity ratio used to time Treasuries (Ch 5, Ch 6)
**Disinversion** — the yield curve turning positive again after inverting (Ch 3)
**Early / late recession** — the split of the recession regime. Risky assets do worst early and best late (Ch 3, Ch 15)
**Earnings yield gap** — equity earnings yield minus the 10-year yield (Ch 15)
**ERI** — Citi earnings revision index, a breadth measure of analyst revisions (Ch 8, Ch 10)
**Excess return** — return over T-bills, the book's default measure (Ch 2)
**Fallen angel** — issuer downgraded from IG to HY (Ch 11)
**Fed put** — equity drawdown at which the Fed cuts. About 25%, or 20% with low inflation (Ch 8)
**Financial accident trigger** — banks underperform the SPX by three standard deviations over three months in a down market (Ch 3)
**FX OIS basis** — cost of USD funding via FX swaps. Not a directional signal (Ch 13)
**Generals rule** — cut equity risk when four of the seven leading large caps break their 200dma (Ch 8)
**Good news / bad news regime** — sign of CESI combined with sign of its correlation with the SPX (Ch 9)
**GPR index** — Caldara-Iacoviello news-based geopolitical risk index (Ch 4)
**Information ratio (IR)** — excess return over volatility, used like a Sharpe ratio (Ch 2)
**Leaders / laggards** — markets that do or do not price cuts when the Fed starts easing (Ch 7)
**LDI funds** — leveraged UK pension gilt holders behind the 2022 gilt sell-off (Ch 7)
**Levered loans** — floating-rate sub-investment-grade loans (Ch 11)
**Li Keqiang index** — rail freight, electricity and loan growth gauge of Chinese activity (Ch 10, Ch 14)
**Marginal cost of production** — theoretical commodity price floor. It moves with the cycle (Ch 14)
**Max-volatility indicator** — maximum one-year z-score across VIX, MOVE, OVX, FX vol and HY spreads (Ch 11, Ch 12)
**Nearest-neighbour regime** — finding the past periods most like today on seven macro variables (Ch 15)
**OAS** — option-adjusted spread, the credit valuation proxy (Ch 11)
**Payer / receiver** — short or long duration through a swap (Ch 5)
**POLLS** — Citi index of Positioning, Optimism, Liquidity, Leverage, Stress (Ch 9)
**PPP** — purchasing power parity, the FX value anchor (Ch 12)
**Regime-agnostic portfolio** — allocation that holds up in every regime (Ch 15)
**Reverse optimization** — solving for the return that justifies a weight (Ch 16)
**Rising star** — issuer upgraded from HY to IG (Ch 11)
**Risk parity** — equal-risk allocation with leveraged bonds (Ch 15)
**Roll yield** — return from a futures contract moving along the curve (Ch 14)
**Sahm rule** — 3m average unemployment 0.5 points above its 12-month low. Lagging (Ch 3)
**SLOOS** — Fed survey of bank lending standards. Leads defaults, lags prices (Ch 11)
**Soft vs hard data** — survey momentum vs activity momentum. Soft below hard is a risk-off warning (Ch 3)
**Spread return** — credit return after hedging Treasury duration (Ch 11)
**Supply fallacy** — the belief that issuance forecasts predict credit returns (Ch 11)
**Unified government** — one party holds the White House and Congress. Bearish for Treasuries (Ch 4)
**Unsmoothing** — removing autocorrelation from private asset returns (Ch 16)
**USD smirk** — the authors' version of the USD smile: reliable USD strength only in weak growth (Ch 12)
**Volatility targeting** — scaling exposure inversely to realized volatility (Ch 11, Ch 15)
**VRP** — volatility risk premium, VIX minus one-month realized volatility (Ch 9)
