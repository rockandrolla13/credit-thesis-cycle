## Decision rules
- Recession suspected: avoid equities 2 to 3 months before, stay bearish for the first half, wait for Fed cuts. Yields peak a year early only if inflation is low (Ch 3).
- Geopolitical shock: no oil impact, fade fast. Hedge with energy, then gold (Ch 4).
- Market prices a hard landing but SPX is well above its 200dma: distrust the cuts (Ch 5).
- Inflation high: discount inversion signals, cyclicals vs defensives, and receiving at the last hike (Ch 3, 5, 6).
- Fed hiking into inflation: gold, TIPS, Bitcoin stop hedging. Hold CTAs, carry, quality (Ch 15).
- Bubble: buy on entry, sell after the fourth general fails (Ch 8).
- Credit: hold spread risk only with max-vol z-score below 2 and SLOOS loosening. ISM above 50, HY over IG. Buy fallen angels. Ignore supply (Ch 11).
- Intervention: follow only if rates or equities confirm (Ch 13).
- Global easing: receive leaders, not laggards (Ch 7).

## What does not work
Sahm rule and first negative NFP for equities. CAPE and earnings yield gap timing. Breadth for tops. Buybacks. Taylor rules. Trading Fed pauses. 2s/10s inversion as a receive signal. Supply forecasts in credit and curve. IG spread level. Small-cap effect. USD smile right side. FX basis. Gold as inflation hedge. Fading CTAs. Volatility targeting in credit and commodities.

## Tells
- Soft data momentum below hard data: trouble.
- 5s/30s flattening and 2y breakevens falling in a sell-off: yield peak near.
- HY spreads wider before a VIX jump: deeper equity pullback.
- A market that cannot fall on bad news has priced it. Full intraday reversal in USDMXN: active IR 1.41 over 9 days.
- Good data with a negative data-equity correlation: worst SPX regime (0.20).
- Volatility spike plus crowded positioning: the only real carry killer.
