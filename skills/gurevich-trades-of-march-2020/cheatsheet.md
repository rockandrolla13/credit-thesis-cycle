# Cheatsheet: The Trades of March 2020 (Gurevich)

## Decision rules
| When | Do | Because | Ch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crisis with variables you cannot judge | Trade only what must be true eventually; size to survive | You cannot time it | Intro |
| Stocks fall on higher rates and on strong data | Treat the bond sell-off as self-limiting; expect lower rates | Inflation fear regime | 1 |
| The best expression of a view is a bad trade | Re-examine the view | Dominance corollary | 1 |
| Last hike is behind you and the market calls it a "pause" | Price the first cut sooner | Hike-to-ease gap was 1 to 18 months | 2 |
| Fed Funds prices a 5% to 10% hike chance before a meeting | Buy the future | True odds "actually zero" | 2 |
| Emergency intermeeting cut | Buy the next contract at once | Six of six precedents eased again | 4 |
| Price just jumped past your level | Judge only the price in front of you | "Forget the past" | 4 |
| Main leg paid, offset leg has not lost | Close the offset first | Before the market catches on | 4 |
| Bonds extended but cheap to swaps | Pay swaps against them | Cuts rate risk, adds spread risk | 4 |
| Long-horizon asset is cheap and falling | Add at preset levels up to core; no catalyst needed | Waiting misses the move | 5 |
| Trade needs normalization by a date | Keep it small or skip it | "Eventually" is right; "on a deadline" is not | 5, 6 |
| Untenable drawdown | Cut the positions causing it | Selective cuts backfire | 6 |
| Portfolio fine, one loser | Decide on that trade alone | Cutting is not required | 6 |
| Target reached on confirming news | Take the target | Your edge is now public | 6 |
| Forced cutting is over | Re-ask the odds before expiry; sell if bleak | Voluntary, not forced | 7 |
| Missed the dislocation | Do not chase in size | XMA, GDXJ losses | 8 |
| Tempted to hold cash or short after liquidity turns | Treat cash as an asset; check its price | Beanie Baby Theory | Concl |

## Instrument choice
| Situation | Expression | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Wide flat outcome range, boundary on one side, survival at stake | Deep OTM long-dated option plus small outright core | Decay; freeze past strike |
| Normal directional view | Outright futures | Options are a "seductive lure" |
| Unlikely event priced at zero cost | Outright front contract (free lunch) | Accept noise |
| Extended duration, cheap spread | Asset swap / invoice spread | Margin and funding |
| Long rates overshot in a panic | Payer swaption | Pricing and margin work |
| Spot shock priced along the curve | Deferred future | Patience |
| Long USD view | The high-beta currency that falls in more scenarios (AUD) | Cover in increments |

## Thresholds and numbers
- Black swan: 1% to 2% chance in any month; about 100 bp move.
- LIBOR-OIS: about 20 bp in normal times.
- Hike fade: no value near 60/40; value near 90/10.
- EDM0 98.375 call: bought at 0.06; closed at 1.326.
- Leverage trap: 10:1 on a 5% assumed loss is 50%; a 12% loss is 120%.
- TIPS asset swap: 28 bp low, 92 bp entry, 2.50% "minimum economic value".
- September 2019: he cut to about 50% of maximum risk after a strong run.
- AUD residual stops: 0.60 for half, 0.65 for the rest.

## Tells
- Hedge options "dirt" cheap while an event develops: it is not priced.
- Dollar and bonds weak together, then bottoming together: late cycle.
- LIBOR sets higher while the Fed eases: funding squeeze, not a signal to fade with short-dated contracts.
- Bonds and stocks fall together; gold falls too: dollar shortage and forced selling.
- A breakeven or spread implies something absurd: forced flow, look for carry.
- Record move in a sovereign bond on dovish news: buyers are out of capacity.
- You are out of risk capacity: so are most others; stops are near exhaustion.
- "Good news is good news" again; chat goes quiet: the regime has turned.

## Risk architecture
- Portfolio rules are rigid. Position exits are strategy.
- Core is well below maximum. At maximum you can only hope.
- Hold reserves so the bottom finds you with capacity.
- Decide in calm; execute the plan in the storm. "Weak opinions, strongly held."
