# Glossary: Investing Amid Low Expected Returns (Ilmanen)

**Active return** — manager return minus benchmark return, with no beta adjustment (Ch 7)
**Adding-up constraint** — every buyer has a seller, so aggregate preference changes move valuations, not net flows (Ch 8)
**Alpha** — return unexplained by the chosen factor model. No unique measure exists (Ch 7, Ch 10)
**Alpha decay** — fall in measured alpha over time, or the speed at which a signal's edge fades (Ch 7, Ch 15)
**Alternative risk premia (ARP)** — publicly known long/short style strategies with positive expected reward (Ch 6)
**Asness Law** — no product is so good that high enough fees cannot make it a bad investment (Ch 15)
**BAB (bet against beta)** — market-neutral factor long levered low-beta stocks and short de-levered high-beta stocks (Ch 6)
**Bad returns in bad times** — the property that earns a risk premium (Ch 8)
**Bond risk premium (BRP)** — excess return of a long government bond over cash. Also term premium (Ch 4)
**Break-even spread** — the credit spread that just offsets expected default losses (Ch 4)
**CAEY** — cyclically adjusted earnings yield, 1/CAPE. Used as a real expected return proxy (Ch 2, Ch 4, Ch 16)
**Carry** — an asset's return if capital market conditions stay unchanged (Ch 6)
**Craftsmanship alpha** — small edges from design and implementation choices (Ch 11)
**Credit premium** — excess return of corporate bonds over duration-matched government bonds (Ch 4)
**Defensive style** — favouring low-risk or high-quality assets within an asset class (Ch 6)
**Demystifying** — regressing a manager's returns on market and style factors to split beta, ARP and alpha (Ch 7)
**Discount rate effect** — a fall in required return raises realized return now and lowers expected return after (Ch 2, Ch 10)
**Dry powder** — committed private capital not yet invested (Ch 5)
**Dusty corners** — less-covered markets with more alpha dispersion and higher fees (Ch 7)
**ESG-Sharpe ratio frontier** — best Sharpe ratio available at each ESG score. Hump-shaped (Ch 14)
**Fallen angels** — bonds just downgraded from investment grade to high yield, sold under forced pressure (Ch 4)
**Fast pain / slow pain** — quick repricing losses versus decades of low income (Ch 2, Ch 18)
**FLAM** — fundamental law of active management: IR = IC × √N × TC (Ch 10)
**Funding ratio** — pension assets divided by liabilities (Ch 3)
**Glide path** — planned change in equity weight with age (DC) or funding ratio (DB) (Ch 3)
**Illiquidity premium** — extra return for holding assets that are costly or slow to sell (Ch 5)
**Implementation shortfall** — market impact plus pre-trade opportunity cost (Ch 15)
**Inflation hedge versus inflation protection** — short-term correlation with inflation surprises versus a long-run real premium (Ch 4)
**Information ratio (IR)** — beta-adjusted active return over tracking error (Ch 10)
**IRR** — internal rate of return, the gameable private-asset performance metric (Ch 5)
**Limits of arbitrage** — costs, risks and constraints that let mispricing persist (Ch 8)
**Line-item thinking** — judging each holding alone instead of by portfolio contribution (Ch 9)
**Maverick risk** — the risk of losing alone because the portfolio differs from peers (Ch 11, Ch 13)
**Momentum crash** — sharp loss in cross-sectional momentum just after a market turn (Ch 6)
**Net total payout yield** — dividend yield plus net buyback yield (Ch 4)
**OAS** — option-adjusted spread over matched-duration Treasuries (Ch 4)
**Outcome bias** — judging a decision by its result, not its process (Ch 1)
**PME** — public-market equivalent, a private equity performance measure against a public index (Ch 5)
**QMJ (quality minus junk)** — dollar-neutral factor on profitability, growth and safety (Ch 6)
**Rearview-mirror expectations** — extrapolating past realized returns (Ch 2)
**Rebalancing timing luck** — sensitivity of results to arbitrary rebalance dates (Ch 11, Ch 16)
**Risk parity** — equal risk allocation across lowly correlated asset classes, usually levered (Ch 11, Ch 12)
**Sharpe ratio (SR)** — average excess return over cash divided by volatility (Ch 1)
**Sin premium** — higher expected return on assets that ESG investors shun (Ch 14)
**Slow beta** — smoothed private assets show equity beta only to persistent market moves (Ch 5)
**Smart beta** — long-only portfolio with style tilts (Ch 6)
**Smoothing service** — the comfort of unmarked returns, which investors pay for with a lower premium (Ch 5)
**Spread capture** — realized credit excess return as a share of average spread (Ch 4)
**Tangency portfolio** — the maximum Sharpe ratio portfolio of risky assets (Ch 12)
**Transfer coefficient (TC)** — correlation between the ideal unconstrained portfolio and the actual one (Ch 10)
**Trend following** — time-series momentum: long assets with positive recent return, short negative (Ch 6, Ch 13)
**Value spread** — ratio of cheap-side to rich-side valuation in a value portfolio (Ch 6)
**Variance drain** — gap between arithmetic and geometric mean, about half the variance (Ch 4)
**Volatility targeting** — scaling positions inversely to estimated volatility (Ch 13)
**Windfall gain** — the part of realized return caused by a fall in the asset's own yield (Ch 2)
