# Section VI: Policy and Research Questions

**Type**: METHOD (what to measure and disclose) | **Source pages**: 13-15

## Core Idea
The capital structure of the industry is still forming. The first job is to measure and disclose, not to restrict.

## Four question sets (Sec VI)
1. **Measurement.**
   - Physical capital is spread across buildings, power, networking and chips.
   - Financial exposure is spread across corporate balance sheets, leases, joint ventures, guarantees, project debt and SPVs.
   - "Reported hyperscaler capital expenditures therefore capture only part of the buildout."
   - Identify who legally owns each asset. Also identify who bears demand, refinancing and residual-value risk.
   - Work in progress: Van Nieuwerburgh and Su (2026), "Clouds of credit". Private providers such as Atriumdata.ai.
2. **Disclosure and accounting.** The author's list of what better disclosure would map:
   - project-level debt
   - equity interests in joint ventures
   - future lease commitments
   - residual-value guarantees
   - construction guarantees
   - tenant concentration
   - renewal options
   - assumed recovery values of specialised assets

   The goal is not to end off-balance-sheet financing, which "can serve legitimate economic purposes". The goal is transparency.
3. **Valuation and capital-structure design.**
   - Long-lived buildings and power systems sit with hardware whose economic life "may be much shorter and more uncertain".
   - Open questions: depreciation, collateral values, lease duration, refinancing risk, the right maturity of debt.
   - There is an incentive to separate infrastructure-heavy assets from high-growth operating companies to protect reported capital intensity and valuation multiples.
4. **Financial stability.**
   - "It would be premature to conclude that AI infrastructure already poses systemic risk comparable to earlier credit booms."
   - Ask how much exposure banks, insurers, private credit funds, securitisation vehicles and other institutions hold.
   - Ask how concentrated those claims are in the same tenants, and how sensitive they are to AI demand forecasts.
   - Ask how losses spread under a **joint decline in utilisation, collateral values and refinancing capacity**.
   - Off-balance-sheet structures matter "not because they necessarily increase aggregate risk, but because they may make correlated exposures harder to observe before a downturn".

## Closing position (Sec VI)
The open research question is when financial innovation "improves risk sharing rather than obscuring leverage and tail risk exposure". The author states no conflicts of interest.
