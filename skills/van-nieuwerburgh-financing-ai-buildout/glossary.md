# Glossary: Financing the AI Buildout (Van Nieuwerburgh)

**Asset-level leverage**: debt against the specific infrastructure asset, as opposed to the hyperscaler's reported corporate leverage (Sec III)
**Asset-light corporate profile**: low reported capital intensity and leverage, which Meta preserves by placing Hyperion in a separate vehicle (Sec IV)
**Bankruptcy-remote special-purpose vehicle (SPV)**: an entity that issues debt backed by lease payments and isolates the project from the tenant's balance sheet (Sec IV)
**Beignet**: the Meta and Blue Owl joint venture that raised $27bn of debt for Hyperion in October 2025 (Sec IV)
**Behind-the-meter power**: generation the developer builds on site instead of taking power from the grid (App A)
**Capacity-weighted non-realisation**: share of pipeline GW never completed, including downsizing. 43.2% in the central scenario (App B)
**Capital-recovery factor a(r, N)**: constant annual cash flow per dollar of capital whose present value over N years is one dollar (App D)
**Capital base (B_t)**: cohort cost times phi, the cost grossed up for capital committed during construction (App D)
**Circularity**: mutual dependence of chip designers, clouds, model developers, operators and investors through contracts and financing (Sec V)
**Cleanview**: the project-level US data-centre database the author uses, accessed September 12, 2026 (App B)
**Cluster-equivalent rack**: a rack count that allocates critical IT load over fully configured compute, including network and storage power (App A, footnote 10)
**Completion cohort**: all capacity that becomes operational in a given year, valued at that year's cost per MW (App A, App B)
**Critical IT capacity**: power available to IT equipment. Total facility power divided by PUE (Sec II)
**Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)**: cash flow available over debt service. 1.12x for Hyperion (Sec IV)
**Duan smearing**: the correction the author applies when converting fitted log capacity to levels. Factor 1.1313 (App B)
**GB300 NVL72**: the NVIDIA rack-scale system used as the cost unit. About 142 kW and $4.0m per rack (App A)
**Hyperion**: Meta's data-centre campus of about 2.0 GW and about $30bn, excluding IT equipment (Sec IV)
**Hyperscalers**: in this paper, Oracle, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta and Alphabet (Sec III)
**Lease-adjusted leverage (L2, L4)**: leverage with commenced leases plus 0.75 times uncommenced leases added to debt (Sec IV, footnote 6)
**Lease optionality**: the tenant's right to terminate at each four-year renewal date (Sec IV)
**Mature annual revenue**: revenue once all 2025-32 cohorts are operational (App D)
**Operating cash-flow margin (mu)**: operating cash flow over revenue. 50% in the central case (App D)
**Originate-to-distribute**: lending made to be sold on, which the author says can weaken monitoring (Sec V)
**phi**: construction-carry factor. 1.04527 at a 10% cost of capital (App D)
**Power usage effectiveness (PUE)**: total facility power over critical IT power. 1.2 assumed (Sec II, App A)
**Realisation rate**: assumed share of announced capacity completed by 2032 or after 2032 (App B)
**Residual value guarantee (RVG)**: the tenant's promise to cover the shortfall between sale proceeds and a contractual minimum value if it terminates the lease (Sec IV)
**Sopaipilla**: 960 MW Meta and BlackRock project in El Paso, Texas. $12.3bn of A+ bonds in July 2026 (Sec IV, footnote 7)
**Uncommenced lease**: a signed future lease that GAAP does not yet put on the balance sheet (Sec IV)
**Unlevered return (r)**: required return on the asset before financing. 10% in the central case (App D)
