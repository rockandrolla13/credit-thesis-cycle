# Glossary: Bank Lending to Private Credit (FEDS Notes)

Cites: "Sec" = main note. "C Sec" = companion note.

**BDC (Business Development Company)**: closed-end investment company that lends to small and mid-sized US businesses. Must invest at least 70 percent of assets in eligible portfolio companies. Statutory leverage up to 2:1 debt to equity (C Sec 4.2). Files 10-Qs and 10-Ks (Sec 2.2)
**BDC Collateral**: LSEG LPC data platform built from BDC 10-Qs and 10-Ks. The companion calls it "proprietary" (C Sec 4.2)
**BDC types**: publicly traded, public non-traded, private (C Sec 4.2). The Z.1 memo lines call them traded perpetual, private perpetual, private finite (C Table 3)
**CET1 ratio**: common equity tier 1 capital over risk-weighted assets. 13.02 percent for Y-14 banks in the stress table (Sec 4.2, Table 3)
**Committed amount**: the full size of a credit facility, drawn or not. The $95 billion headline is committed (Sec 3.1)
**Cosine similarity**: pairwise measure of how alike two banks' commitment portfolios are. Higher is more alike. After Girardi et al. 2021 (Sec 4.1)
**Depository institution loans n.e.c.**: the Z.1 liability line that carries bank loans to the private credit vehicles (C Tables 1 to 4)
**Dry powder**: capital committed to a fund but not yet drawn. Not in Z.1 AUM. Shown as a memo item (C fn 9, C Table 2)
**FEDS Note**: article in which Board staff "offer their own views". Shorter and less technical than a working paper (main note disclaimer)
**Form PF**: confidential SEC filing by private fund advisers. Primary source for PD fund balance sheets in the Z.1 (C Sec 4.2)
**FR Y-14Q Schedule H.1 ("Y-14")**: quarterly supervisory form. All corporate loans over $1 million at stress-tested bank holding companies (Sec 2.1)
**From-whom-to-whom data**: sector-level detail on which vehicle type lends to which borrower sector. Available through the Data Download Program and FRED (C Sec 4.3, C fn 14)
**HHI (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index)**: concentration of one bank's commitments across its private credit borrowers. 0 to 1. Higher is more concentrated (Sec 4.1)
**Interval fund / tender offer fund**: registered closed-end funds for illiquid assets. Interval funds must repurchase shares at set intervals. Tender offer funds have discretion (C Sec 4.2)
**LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio)**: banks hold high-quality liquid assets equal to 40 percent of unused credit lines to NBFIs (Sec 4.2, fn 9)
**Lead arranger**: the lender named as arranging a BDC facility. Used to classify lenders by type in the 10-Q data (Sec 3.2)
**LGD**: loss given default. A bank-internal measure reported in Y-14 (Sec 2.1)
**Middle-market CLO (MM CLO)**: collateralized loan obligation backed by loans to middle-market companies. Often managed by the direct lender or an affiliate (C Sec 4.2)
**Middle-market firm**: annual EBITDA between $5 million and $100 million (fn 5)
**NBFI**: nonbank financial intermediary (Sec 1)
**Other NBFIs**: the main note's comparison group. All NBFI borrowers in Y-14 other than BDCs and PD funds (Sec 3)
**PD (probability of default)**: a bank's own estimate, reported in Y-14 (Sec 2.1). Do not confuse with PD fund
**PD fund (private debt fund)**: privately offered closed-end vehicle, usually a limited partnership. Capital is committed and called over time. Investors typically cannot redeem early (C Sec 4.2)
**Private credit (main note)**: "direct loans made to mid-market businesses typically by non-bank vehicles" (Sec 1)
**Private credit (companion)**: "privately negotiated loans to businesses that are originated by nonbank investment funds" (C Sec 1)
**Private credit vehicles**: BDCs and PD funds in the main note (Sec 1). The companion adds interval and tender offer funds and MM CLOs (C Sec 2)
**Private debt fund shares**: new Z.1 instrument, table F522.3.s. Net asset value of deployed capital (C Sec 4.3)
**ROW (Rest of the World)**: Z.1 sector that carries foreign-domiciled PD funds and MM CLOs (C Sec 3)
**Synthetic risk transfer**: structure in which a bank passes the riskiest part of a loan exposure to private credit funds and other investors (Sec 5)
**TIN**: taxpayer identification number. First key used to find BDCs in Y-14 (Sec 2.1)
**Utilization rate**: drawn amount over committed amount (Sec 3.1)
**Utilized amount**: the drawn part of a facility. The $56 billion figure is utilized (Sec 3.1)
**Warehouse financing**: bank lending that funds loans before they move to a private credit manager or vehicle. Named as a growing channel (Sec 5)
**Z.1 / Financial Accounts of the United States**: the Fed's integrated sector balance sheets. Private credit added from the September 2026 release (C Sec 1)
