# Section 6: Conclusion (main note)

Source: main note, Section 6.

## Core Idea
The authors restate the results and name the two things to keep watching: BDC leverage and bank partnerships. They also state that the regulatory data limit what can be known.

## The conclusions, in the note's order
1. Bank credit to private credit vehicles is "growing fast but still small relative to other NBFIs".
2. "the financial stability implications seem limited".
3. The loans have "higher utilization rates, higher interest rates, lower probability of default, and lower delinquency rates" than loans to other NBFIs.
4. US banks "remain well capitalized and highly liquid, allowing them to absorb a significant drawdown risk from private credit vehicles".
5. Watch item: "public BDCs' increasing use of leverage compared with other NBFIs".
6. Watch item: banks "increasingly expanding their strategic partnerships with private credit vehicles".
7. "These developments and the increasing interconnectedness warrant close monitoring."

## The stated data limit
The authors "recognize the limitations of current regulatory data, and the difficulties associated with identifying banks' exposures to private credit in these data". They expect "ongoing efforts by the banking agencies implementing changes to reporting requirements" to help.
