## Core Idea
The book is an edited volume on forecasting expected returns. It covers "established topics in the forecasting literature" and "a number of new ones". Each chapter has its own authors and its own notation. The editor groups the chapters into six themes.

## Key Concepts
- **Black-Litterman model**: the editor calls it "a methodology of great importance to practitioners", because it combines analysts' views with equilibrium modelling.
- **Ranked returns**: forecasts stated as an order, not as numbers (chapters 4 and 5).
- **Binomial world**: the editor calls it "a very simple framework" that still gives "important insights".

## What the editor says is missing
"There is more that has not been included." The editor hopes to add it later. The book states no unifying model and no summary chapter.

## Key Takeaways
- Treat each chapter as a separate paper. Symbols such as omega, lambda, alpha and beta change meaning between chapters.
- The book is about equity returns, portfolio construction and option prices. No chapter models credit spreads, bond returns or central banks. Chapter 1 mentions bond yield spreads only as predictors of stock returns.
