# Introduction

**Author**: Isaac Souede, Permal Group (pp. xvii-xx)

## Core Idea
The book is a practitioner survey of global macro, commissioned by Permal Group for institutional investors who could not find an "institutional quality" guide. Each contributor covers one aspect. This chapter is thin. It is a map of the book, not a method.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The book's design**: a cross-section of practitioners. The list is discretionary and systematic managers, macro strategists, a risk manager, a prime broker, investment consultants, fund-of-funds managers and institutional investors.
- **Chapter map** (as the introduction states it):
  - Ch 1 history and three future developments. Ch 2 running a discretionary firm. Ch 3 systematic macro. Ch 4 GTAA versus CTAs.
  - Ch 5 the macro strategist, with a case study. Ch 6 emerging markets, turning top-down scenarios into trade ideas.
  - Ch 7 risk management, VaR and stress tests. Ch 8 geopolitical risk.
  - Ch 9 prime brokerage. Ch 10 leverage.
  - Ch 11 the consultant's view. Ch 12 the fund-of-funds view.
  - Ch 13 allocating to external macro managers. Ch 14 running macro in-house at a large asset owner.

## Key Concepts
- **Permal Group**: sponsor of the book. Founded 1973. It reports about US$8 billion in global macro funds and accounts, about 40% of its assets, as of September 2012.
- **Permal's flagship fund of macro funds**: launched 1992. It grew to 16 managers and US$700 million in ten years, then to nearly 50 managers and about US$5 billion. It added natural resources and relative value sub-allocations.
- **Managed accounts**: Permal invests a major part through them. The introduction lists the claimed benefits: full transparency, more liquidity, better governance, tailored exposures.

## Anti-patterns
- **Reading the book as neutral**: the sponsor runs a large fund of macro funds. Several chapters argue for allocating to macro. Read the allocation chapters (11 to 14) with that in mind. This caution is the skill's, drawn from the introduction's own disclosure of Permal's business.

## Key Takeaways
1. Use the chapter map to pick the right contributor. The chapters do not share one method.
2. Renewed institutional interest dates from the 2007-09 crisis, per the introduction.
